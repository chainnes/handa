# Staffing ratios

Crew size follows the service style. The service fee on the quotation already pays for this crew. Do not add their wages a second time.

## Buffet

One cook per 40 guests, one server per 25 guests, one captain when the count is 50 or more, and at least one steward. A driver is required outside Quezon City, and a second driver from 160 guests.

## Plated

One cook per 30 guests and one server per 15 guests. Plated service needs a captain from 40 guests and a steward per 60 guests.

## Drop-off

One cook per 50 guests, one steward, no floor servers, and a driver outside Quezon City or from 80 guests. Drop-off does not include chafing dishes.

## Call times

Cooks call in 6 hours before service, or 8 hours before when the count is 100 or more. Servers and the captain call in about 2 hours before. Drivers and stewards call in 3 hours before, for the load.
