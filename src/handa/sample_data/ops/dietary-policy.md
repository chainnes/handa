# Dietary policy

Mesa Clara does not guess about pork, beef, seafood, or vegetarian plates. The brief is the rule.

## Pork and lechon

Lechon belly is pork. If the brief excludes pork, lechon belly is off the menu, including as a centerpiece. Replace it with chicken inasal. Do not serve pork "on the side" for everyone else when the client has excluded pork for the whole event. A debut that allows pork may keep lechon belly on the first pass.

## Beef and seafood

If the brief excludes beef, beef kaldereta comes off and chicken afritada replaces it. If the brief excludes seafood, fish sarciado and grilled tanigue come off. Grilled tanigue is replaced with chicken inasal. Fish sarciado is replaced with chicken afritada.

## Vegetarian plates

Vegetarian guests receive tofu adobo in place of the meat courses. They still receive rice, the vegetable, dessert, and a drink. Count vegetarian plates once, not once per meat dish. A vegetarian headcount cannot be larger than the guest count. Lumpia sauce in this kitchen is garlic-soy, not peanut, unless a brief explicitly asks for the classic peanut sauce.

## Allergens to print on the ticket

Print allergens from the price book on every line: fish, soy, gluten, dairy, egg. Do not invent an allergen that the price book does not list.
