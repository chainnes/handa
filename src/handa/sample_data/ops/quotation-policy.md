# Quotation policy

The price book owns the pesos. A manager owns the signature.

## Margin floors

Corporate events are priced at a 26% margin. Debut events at 28%. Weddings at 32%. Fiestas at 24%. Overhead is 10% of food, service, equipment, and travel. The quote is cost divided by one minus the margin floor. Do not type a different total into the client note.

## Budget fit

If the quote is at or under the client's budget, the file can go to the manager to be issued. If it is over, the desk runs a cost-down pass: premium dishes swap to house dishes first, and on the third pass the service style drops one step (plated to buffet, buffet to drop-off). After that, stop. Issuing a quote that is still over the budget is an exception. It asks the client to raise what they can spend. It does not discount the price book.

## Human approval

Every quotation stays a draft until a manager approves it. Sending a file back runs another pass and keeps the manager's note on the client note. Cancelling closes the file. Nothing in this binder is a live client price until that approval happens.
