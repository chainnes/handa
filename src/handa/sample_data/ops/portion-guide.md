# Portion guide

Portions come from the guest count. Do not round a banquet by feel.

## Rice and drink buffer

Rice and drinks include a 5% buffer, rounded up to a whole portion. A lunch for 60 guests gets 63 portions of garlic rice and 63 drinks. Other courses are one portion per guest who eats them. Meat courses are portioned for guests who are not on the vegetarian count.

## What a plate contains

A corporate buffet plate is garlic rice, one viand, chopsuey, buko pandan, and iced tea. A debut plate adds a centerpiece. A wedding plate is plated and can carry two viands. A fiesta plate adds pancit guisado.

The buffer is only for rice and drinks. Do not add 5% to lechon belly, beef, or dessert.
