# Suppliers and lead times

Order from the price book supplier. Do not invent a vendor.

## Balintawak Market

Proteins, vegetables, buko, eggs, and calamansi. Lead time is 1 day. A same-day event is already late for Balintawak. Say so on the file. Do not pretend the order can still make a morning market run.

## Nova Wholesale QC

Rice, sauces, dairy, noodles, and drink bases. Lead time is 1 day. Same-day events miss Nova as well.

## Mila's Lechon, Quezon City

Lechon belly is bought cooked, by the portion, not roasted in our kitchen. Lead time is 2 days. If the event is sooner than that, flag Mila's on the purchase list and do not promise lechon belly.

Travel outside Quezon City adds a ₱900 fee and a driver. Quezon City venues do not.
