# Signature spreads

These are the first-pass menus. Cost-down passes may swap the premium dishes. The names below are the price-book names.

## Corporate buffet

Garlic rice, chicken afritada, chopsuey, buko pandan, and iced tea. Tofu adobo covers vegetarian plates. This spread has no pork, so a no-pork brief does not change the first pass.

## Debut buffet

Java rice, lechon belly, beef kaldereta, lumpiang sariwa, buko salad, and calamansi juice. Lechon belly, beef kaldereta, buko salad, and calamansi juice are the premium items. The house swap is chicken inasal, chicken afritada, buko pandan, and iced tea.

## Wedding plated

Garlic rice, beef kaldereta, grilled tanigue, chopsuey, leche flan, and calamansi juice. Grilled tanigue is the seafood premium. The house swap is chicken afritada, fish sarciado, buko pandan, and iced tea.

## Fiesta buffet

Java rice, chicken inasal, pancit guisado, chopsuey, buko pandan, and iced tea. Pancit guisado in this kitchen is the tofu version, not a pork pancit.
